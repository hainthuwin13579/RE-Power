ABOUT RE:POWER

Repair more. Waste less. Trust your choices.

re:power helps people explore electronics repair options, compare available information and connect with repair shops. Our goal is to make repair easier to understand, help people make informed spending decisions and encourage responsible use of electronic devices.

OUR PURPOSE

Making electronics repair easier to explore

When a device stops working, people may be unsure where to find a repair service, what a repair might cost or whether a provider supports their device.

re:power aims to bring useful repair information together so customers can explore available options and contact providers directly before deciding what to do.

Encourage repair

Help people discover repair options before automatically replacing a device.

Support informed spending

Present available service and indicative pricing information while encouraging customers to obtain a final quote from the repair provider.

Promote responsible reuse

Encourage suitable repairs, longer device use and responsible electronic-waste handling.

SHOP INFORMATION

What does shop verification mean?

Accurate business information helps customers decide which providers to contact. re:power's intended review process focuses on checking listing details and recording when those details were last reviewed.

Prototype disclosure: Sample listings on this website have not necessarily been checked against real business records. A sample listing must not be treated as a verified shop.

Business identity and contact details

The intended review checks the supplied business name and available contact information.

Supported devices and services

Listing information should describe the device types and repair services the provider says it offers.

Location and service area

The intended review checks the published shop address or service area, where this information is available.

Last reviewed date

A listing should show when its details were last reviewed, where a review record exists.

Clear listing labels

Labels such as "Verified" should only be used when the stated review has actually been completed. A listing review is not a guarantee of repair quality.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Pricing and sponsored listings

Customers should understand what listing prices mean, what may change after diagnosis and how paid promotion is distinguished from ordinary listings.

Indicative repair prices

Prices displayed on re:power may be estimates or indicative ranges based on available listing information. They may not include every part, labour charge or additional service.

  • Ask whether diagnosis fees apply.
  • Confirm parts, labour and warranty terms.
  • Request the final quote before authorising work.

Collection and delivery

Collection or return delivery may be available from selected providers, but it is not automatically included in the repair price.

  • Confirm the delivery area.
  • Ask about collection and return fees.
  • Agree on timing and handover arrangements.

HOW REFERRALS WORK

Transparent referrals. Clear agreements.

re:power may receive an agreed commission from participating repair shops for qualifying completed jobs referred through the platform. The applicable commission, eligibility conditions, reporting process and payment timing should be agreed with each participating shop in advance.

1

Customer discovers a repair provider through re:power.

2

Customer contacts the provider and agrees on the repair.

3

The provider completes the agreed repair.

4

Participating parties reconcile eligible referrals under their agreed terms.

A referral commission is a business arrangement between re:power and a participating provider. Customers should confirm the final repair price directly with the shop. The existence of a referral relationship does not guarantee a particular repair result.

Understand the intended limits of the prototype and the responsibilities involved in arranging a repair.

Privacy Terms of Use Repair Safety Delivery
Privacy and data handling

re:power should collect only the information needed for its stated functions. For example, a working enquiry form may require contact details to respond to a request.

The production website should explain what information is collected, why it is needed, who receives it, how long it is retained and how users can make relevant privacy requests.

In this front-end prototype, forms may demonstrate validation without transmitting or storing submitted information. Actual handling depends on the backend and any connected third-party services.

Do not submit passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through a demonstration form.

Terms of use and service limitations

re:power is intended to help users discover repair services and review available provider information. Listings, prices, service descriptions and availability may change and should be confirmed directly with the relevant business.

Repair shops are independent providers. Customers should review the proposed work, final price, parts, turnaround time, warranty and payment terms before authorising a repair.

This prototype does not establish a binding repair contract, guarantee provider availability or promise a specific repair outcome. Production terms should be reviewed and adapted to applicable law before launch.

Repair safety and customer responsibilities

Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions and seek a suitably qualified technician when a repair requires specialist tools or electrical expertise.

Do not open mains-powered appliances, handle exposed wiring, or attempt repairs involving damaged batteries unless you are appropriately trained and equipped. Stop using equipment that shows signs of overheating, smoke, swelling or electrical damage.

Before handing over a device, back up important files where possible, sign out of personal accounts and discuss data protection with the repair provider. Keep a record of the device's condition and the agreed repair.

Customers should confirm any warranty or data-loss implications directly with the manufacturer or repair provider.

Collection, delivery and handover

Collection and return delivery may be offered by selected providers. Availability, service areas, charges and timing depend on the provider and the agreed arrangement.

Before arranging collection, confirm the collection address, contact details, delivery fee, expected turnaround, handover procedure and responsibility for the device during transit.

Record the device's condition and retain any collection receipt or tracking reference supplied by the provider. Do not assume that transit damage, loss or repair failure is insured.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about re:power

Find out what the platform does and what to confirm before arranging a repair.

Does re:power repair devices directly?

re:power is designed to help users find and compare independent repair providers. The selected provider carries out the repair under the agreement made directly with the customer.

Are all repair shops verified?

Not necessarily. The prototype may contain sample listings that have not been verified. A live website should explain the checks completed for each listing and display verification labels only when supported by an actual review record.

Are the listed repair prices final?

No. Displayed prices may be indicative. The provider should confirm the diagnosis, parts, labour, additional fees and final quote before you authorise the repair.

Does re:power receive a commission?

re:power may receive an agreed commission from participating providers for qualifying completed referrals. The specific terms depend on the agreement with each provider.

Does re:power provide insurance for my device?

No insurance or repair protection is represented as an active re:power service here. Any future coverage offering would need to be provided by an authorised provider under its own terms.

How can I report incorrect shop information?

Contact the support team and include the listing name, the issue you noticed and any relevant public evidence. Do not include passwords or payment details.
