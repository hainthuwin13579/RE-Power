BEFORE PICKUP

Helpful collection tips

  • Back up important files where possible.
  • Photograph the device and accessories before handover.
  • Remove personal SIM or memory cards if appropriate.
  • Record existing scratches or damage with the collector.
  • Keep your enquiry or handover reference.
CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Safety, handling & responsibility

Know what happens before your device leaves your hands.

Condition record

The collector and customer should record the device condition, visible damage, accessories and handover time before transfer.

Careful carrying

Devices are packed and carried according to their size, weight and fragility. Large or unusually heavy items require prior assessment.

Protection terms

Any damage claim must follow the written terms confirmed at booking, including declared value, evidence and claim deadlines.

High-floor access

Upper-floor collection can be requested. Lift access, stair access, building rules and any additional fee are checked before confirmation.
