SIMPLE AND TRANSPARENT

How the Referral Process Works

A clear process helps customers, repair shops and re:power understand their responsibilities.

01

Customer Discovers

A customer finds a suitable partner shop through re:power's repair directory.

02

Customer Contacts

The customer contacts the shop to discuss the device, issue, estimated price and repair arrangements.

03

Repair Is Completed

The shop handles the repair and confirms completion through the agreed process.

04

Referrals Are Reconciled

The parties review eligible referrals and settle any agreed commission according to their agreement.

Clear terms, agreed in advance

Referral eligibility, commission rates, payment timing, reporting and responsibilities must be agreed separately. Listing a business does not automatically guarantee referrals, sales or a specific income.
