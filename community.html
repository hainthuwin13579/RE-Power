HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about repair services, delivery, community activities and your information.

Are repair estimates guaranteed?

No. Prices displayed on re:power may be indicative and can change after the provider inspects the device. Confirm the final quote, parts, labour, additional fees and any warranty terms directly with the shop before authorising the repair.

Read the pricing policy
Does re:power charge referral commission?

re:power may receive an agreed commission from participating repair shops for qualifying completed referrals. Rates, eligibility and payment timing depend on the relevant business agreement. Customers should confirm repair prices directly with the provider.

Read about referral transparency
Is collection and delivery available everywhere?

Availability depends on the provider, location, device and service arrangement. Contact the relevant provider to confirm the service area, collection time, return arrangements and any additional fees.

Explore Collection & Delivery
How do I register for a community event?

Visit the Community & Donations page and choose an event. If registration is open, follow the event's registration link. Where a Google Form is used, the organiser managing that form receives the submitted registration information.

View Community Activities
Where can I find device donation information?

Visit the Community & Donations page to explore available donation opportunities. Check the receiving organisation's requirements and confirm accepted device types, condition requirements and handover arrangements before donating.

Donation Information
What happens to information entered into this form?

In this front-end prototype, the form performs validation in your browser and shows a demonstration confirmation. It does not send or store your enquiry. A live implementation would need a properly configured backend or form service and an accurate privacy notice.

Read the privacy information
How can I report incorrect information or a website issue?

Select "Report a Problem" in the contact form. Include the relevant page or listing and describe what appears to be incorrect. Do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.
