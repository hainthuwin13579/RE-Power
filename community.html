GIVE DEVICES A SECOND LIFE

Donation opportunities

A usable device may still help someone else. Contact a receiving organisation to confirm its current needs, acceptance criteria and donation process before handing over any equipment.

Check before donating

Acceptance rules vary by organisation. Confirm the accepted device types, condition requirements, collection arrangements and any fees before making a donation.

Device Reuse

Phones, tablets and computers

Ask receiving organisations whether they accept working devices, repairable equipment or devices suitable for parts recovery.

Before handing over a device

  • Confirm the organisation accepts that device type.
  • Back up your personal files.
  • Sign out of accounts and remove personal data safely.
  • Confirm the handover and receipt process.
Donation Information
Electrical Equipment

Household electronics

Some collection programmes may accept selected household electronics. Check their rules for working, damaged or obsolete equipment.

Check these requirements

  • Accepted appliance types and sizes.
  • Whether damaged equipment is permitted.
  • Safe transport and collection arrangements.
  • Whether batteries or accessories must be separated.
Donation Information
Responsible Disposal

Unusable or damaged devices

Equipment that cannot be reused may need specialist electronic-waste handling. Do not assume it can be included in a general donation.

Before arranging disposal

  • Ask about accepted electronic-waste categories.
  • Do not place loose batteries in general rubbish.
  • Do not dismantle hazardous components yourself.
  • Request disposal or collection guidance.
